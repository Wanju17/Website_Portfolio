PCU Canteen Pre-Order & Management System

Transforms campus dining into a queue-free, digital service by replacing traditional cash-and-carry bottlenecks. Students can browse live menu availability, customize meal choices, schedule timed pickups, earn loyalty rewards, and reorder from past history. Built with a responsive frontend and powered by Node.js/Express REST APIs, real-time Firebase Firestore database sync, and Cloudinary image management—backed by kitchen queue dashboards, sales analytics, and role-based administrative controls.
